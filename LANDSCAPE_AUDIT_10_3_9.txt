IKIGAI WORLD 10.3.9 — LANDSCAPE ORIENTATION AUDIT

Scope reviewed: index.html, style.css, classroom.css, arena.css, app.js.

Findings in source 10.3.8:
- Map stage had multiple portrait-only rotate(90deg) rules.
- Room scene had a separate portrait-only rotate(90deg) rule.
- Login, generic modal/activity/chat overlays and the music button did not share one global landscape canvas.
- Arena joystick had its own portrait-specific physical-corner placement and pointer-axis conversion.

Changes in 10.3.9:

- Reset map stage and room scene transforms inside that canvas to prevent double rotation.
- Moved the music toggle inside #game so it follows the same canvas and room mount/restore behavior.
- Applied full-canvas sizing to login, modal, activity, chat and map/room scenes.
- Kept joystick pointer conversion aligned with the single app-canvas rotation and pinned it to the physical bottom-left on portrait-held phones.
- Added best-effort native landscape lock on fullscreenchange; CSS rotation is the fallback.
- Preserved native landscape behavior.

Static checks:
