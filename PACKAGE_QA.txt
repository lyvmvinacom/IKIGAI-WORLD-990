IKIGAI WORLD 9.3 — INDEPENDENT AMBIENT CANVAS
================================================
- Added `canvas#ambientCanvas` as a dedicated transparent overlay.
- Added `ambient-effects.js`; does not modify Supabase, game state, rooms, or hotspot logic.
- Effects: falling sakura petals, waterfall shimmer/particles, fountain spray/ripples, lake glints.
- Canvas uses pointer-events:none and is above building art but below HUD.
- Animation pauses when mapScene is hidden or browser tab is backgrounded.
- Device pixel ratio capped at 1.5; animation throttled to approximately 30 FPS.
- Respects the operating system's prefers-reduced-motion setting.
